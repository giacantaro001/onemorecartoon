Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026 (updated for Android)

One More Cartoon! is designed for parents and does not collect any personal data. We do not have servers that receive information from the app, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Settings such as the number of cartoons, language, routine and child’s name are saved only on your device.
  • Voice recordings (“My voice”) are made with the microphone only when you choose to record, are stored only on your device, and can be deleted at any time in the app. They are never uploaded.
  • Speech uses recordings bundled with the app or your device’s built-in text-to-speech, which works on the device.

Purchases

The unlock (and, on iPhone/iPad, the free trial) are in-app purchases processed by Apple or Google Play. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. The app only asks the store whether the unlock (or trial) is active on your account. On Android the trial start date is stored on your device. See Apple’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is meant to be set up and used by a parent or carer. It collects no information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: smallpeak001@gmail.com.
