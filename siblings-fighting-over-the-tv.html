Siblings fighting over the TV? Fair turns that end the arguments

With two or more children, screen time adds a second battle: who gets to choose. The good news is that the same thing calms both fights. Make the rules visible, make them fair, and make them the same for everyone.

One picker per evening

Instead of arguing over every episode, let one child choose all the cartoons for the evening and the other choose tomorrow. A whole evening feels like a real turn, and there is only one decision to make.

Make the turn fair and visible

Children accept a rule when they can see it is fair: a calendar on the fridge, a coin toss, a spinning wheel. Avoid the same child picking three nights in a row.

Same countdown for everyone

Agree the number of cartoons for all the children together, so nobody is sent to the bath while a sibling keeps watching.

Name every child

When the warning comes, say every child's name, in a different order each time, so nobody always feels second.

Celebrate the ending together

Give the next activity to everyone at once: a race to the bath, a story together, everyone helps with dinner.
