Free trial and unlock

The app is free to download. Tap Start free trial to use everything for 30 days. After that, Unlock forever is a single one-time purchase (no subscription). Payment is handled by Apple and charged to your Apple Account.

New phone or reinstalled the app?

Open the app, tap the trial badge on the setup screen and choose Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. Family Sharing members can restore it too if Family Sharing is on for the purchase.

Refunds

Purchases are made through the App Store, so refunds are handled by Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

The voice is silent

Check the voice switch at the bottom right of the app, the ringer/silent switch and the volume. For “My voice” recordings, allow microphone access in Settings › One More Cartoon!.
