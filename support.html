Free trial and unlock

The app is free to download. Tap Start free trial to use everything for 30 days. After that, Unlock forever is a single one-time purchase (no subscription). Payment is handled by Apple (iPhone/iPad) or Google Play (Android).

New phone or reinstalled the app?

Open the app, tap the trial badge on the setup screen and choose Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account or Google account you bought it with.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google Play at Google Play refunds.

The voice is silent

Check the voice switch at the bottom right of the app, the ringer/silent switch and the volume. For “My voice” recordings, allow microphone access in your phone’s settings for One More Cartoon!.
