How it works

Agree with your child how many cartoons are left. Tap the big yellow button after each one and a cheerful cartoon voice counts down: “Two more cartoons!”, “Last cartoon!”, then “Time for bath!” (or bed, dinner, going outside, or your own message).

  • Big friendly count, dancing TV buddies and a comic finish screen.
  • Your child’s name in the announcements, or record the lines in your own voice.
  • 11 languages: English (UK and US), Italian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Dutch, Polish, Chinese and Japanese.
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking.
  • Free for 30 days, then a single one-time purchase to keep it forever.
