A screen time countdown kids actually understand

“Just one more cartoon!” Sound familiar? One More Cartoon! turns the end of screen time into a game: instead of minutes, your child sees cartoons left: 3… 2… 1. A cheerful voice warns before the last one, then announces what comes next: bath, bed, dinner or going outside.

How it works

  1. Agree how many cartoons are left. Set the total time and how long each cartoon lasts, or just count episodes.
  2. Let the countdown run. A big friendly number and two dancing TV buddies show how many are left; the voice says “Two more cartoons!” and gives a heads-up on the last one.
  3. End with a celebration. “Time for bed, Sam!” A comic finish screen makes the switch to the next activity part of the game, not an argument.

Why parents like it

  • Kids understand “two more cartoons” far better than “ten more minutes”.

Questions parents ask

What is One More Cartoon!?

One More Cartoon! is an iPhone and iPad app (Android coming soon) that helps parents end children's screen time without arguments. Instead of minutes, it counts down in cartoons: a big number, two dancing TV characters and a cheerful voice say "two more cartoons", "last cartoon!" and then what comes next, such as bath time, bedtime or dinner.

What age is it for?

It is made for toddlers and young children, roughly 2 to 7 years old, who can't yet read a clock but can count cartoons. The parent sets it up; the child just watches the countdown.

Why count cartoons instead of minutes?

Young children don't have a sense of time, so "ten more minutes" means nothing to them, but "two more cartoons" is something they can see and agree to. A friendly warning before the last one and a celebration at the end make the switch to the next activity feel like part of the game.

How much does it cost? Is there a subscription?

It's free to download and every feature is free for 30 days. After that, one single purchase unlocks it forever (£1.89 / $1.99 / €1.99). There is no subscription and no ads.

Does it work with YouTube, Netflix or the TV?

Yes. One More Cartoon! doesn't control other apps or the TV. It runs alongside whatever your child is watching, on a phone or tablet propped up nearby, and you tap after each cartoon (or let the timer do it).

Can I use my own voice or my child's name?

Yes. Add your child's name and the voice will say it in the announcements, or record the lines in your own voice. Recordings stay on your device.

Which languages does it speak?

11 languages: English (UK and US), Italian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Dutch, Polish, Chinese and Japanese.

Does it collect any data about my child?

No. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Settings, names and voice recordings stay on your device.

Is it on Android?

The Android version is in testing on Google Play and coming soon.

What devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad (iOS 15 or later), in portrait and landscape. It keeps the screen awake while counting down.
